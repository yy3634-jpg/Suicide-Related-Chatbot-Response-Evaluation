In Cumulative configurations, the L4 (crisis-handling) component most reliably moves responses out of "Critical" or "Empathy-strong, Safety-weak" zones — but only when paired with explicit AI-identity disclosure (L1) does the Empowerment-oriented pattern emerge. Components are not additive: each contributes a specific dimensional improvement.
